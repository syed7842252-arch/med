Clinical information intelligence — not a diagnosis tool

Every fact in the record, traced back to where it came from.

MedLens extracts patient information and lab results from medical reports, structures them, and shows exactly why each value looks the way it does — so a clinician can verify, not guess.

AI extracts → Rules validate → Evidence explains → Humans verify

What it does

Structured Extraction

Pulls patient details and lab values out of uploaded reports into one consistent record instead of loose PDF pages.

Evidence & Provenance

Every fact is tagged with where it came from — typed in by a person, pulled from a report, or produced by the model.

Lab Intelligence

Values are checked against the reference range printed on the report itself — never an assumed medical norm.

Conflict Detection

Contradictions between current and past records — like a new allergy versus "no known allergies" — are flagged, never silently resolved.

Human Verification

Every extracted fact and lab result can be verified, edited, or rejected, with the change kept on record.

Timeline Comparison

Current and previous values sit side by side, in order, so a change is easy to see and never overstated.

Safe AI Summary

A plain-language summary built from the structured record — not the raw PDF — with no diagnosis or treatment advice.

Deterministic Classification

LOW / NORMAL / HIGH is decided by simple comparison against the source range. If no valid range exists, it says so.
